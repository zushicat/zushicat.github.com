05 · Line & Form

Circuit traces, not leader brackets

corners sharp, no shadows anywhere; depth comes from the grayscale register and overlap. Dashed lines are never the primary structure.

09 · Do / Don't

Three tells from the avoid list

✕Rainbow metaball hubs — nodes joined by organic necks with soft shadows.
✓Traces route real flow — orthogonal, elbow radius 12, solder dots, labels attached.
✕Soft drop shadows on circles and cards for fake depth.
✓Flat translucent overlap — register grays at 10–18% build depth honestly.
✕Decorative color — accents that mean nothing and rotate for variety.
✓Color means category — one pop per composition, everything else ink or register.
